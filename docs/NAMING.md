### What "overlay" means
In betting, an **overlay** is a wager where the offered odds are **higher than the true probability warrants** — a positive expected value (+EV) bet. You're being overpaid for the risk.

- Fair coin flip = 50% → fair odds 2.00. Book offers **2.20** → **overlay** (+EV, bet it).
- Book offers **1.80** → **underlay** (−EV, avoid it).

### Why it fits this platform
1. **The name is the metric.** We rank tipsters by CLV — a direct measure of whether their picks were overlays when placed. The brand encodes the thesis.
2. **Self-selects the audience.** Serious, profitable bettors know the term; casuals don't. It attracts the highest-value (paying) users.
3. **Credibility, not hype.** Scam sites shout "GUARANTEED WINS". "Overlay" speaks the language of sharp bettors → instant trust.
4. **Brandable.** One real word, verb-able ("find your overlay"), clean logo/tagline potential.

### Trade-off
"Overlay" is also a common UI/graphics term (CSS/video overlays) → SEO and exact-match `.com` are competitive. Mitigate with a modifier or alt TLD.
