Here’s a comprehensive prioritized list of improvements across UI/feel, usability/experience (for both tipsters and bettors), and sports coverage. I’ve grouped them so you can rank by impact vs effort.
1. Core UX & Information Architecture (Highest leverage for both tipsters and bettors)

Add a proper persistent nav/header + footer (Home, Tipsters/Leaderboard, Sports, How it works / Verification, Pricing or Subscriptions, Login/Signup, Search).
Make the homepage a proper dashboard-style landing: hero with the strong tagline + clear CTAs (Browse tipsters / Get verified), live or recent locked picks feed, top performers by sport/timeframe, “trending” or “rising” sections, and social proof (even if early: “X picks locked this week”).
Leaderboard (/tipsters) currently shows “Loading…” — make it fast, filterable, and rich: sort/filter by CLV, yield/ROI, win rate, # picks, sport, timeframe (7d/30d/90d/all), market type, min sample size. Add sparklines or mini equity curves.
Tipster profile pages need richer content and actions: bio, preferred sports/markets, recent form (last 10–20 picks with results), equity curve / drawdown chart, CLV histogram (you already have distribution), follower/subscriber counts that feel alive, and clear Follow vs Subscribe distinction with benefits explained.
Empty states are currently dead (“No picks yet”). Replace with helpful CTAs, sample data explanations, or “This tipster is just getting started — follow to be notified.”
Add global search (tipsters, sports, markets) and personal dashboard for logged-in users (followed tipsters, active subscriptions, my performance if tipster, notifications).
Mobile-first refinement: larger touch targets, sticky CTAs for Subscribe, readable stats cards.

2. Make the UI feel lively, modern, and trustworthy

Introduce subtle motion: fade/slide-ins on load, hover lifts on cards, progress animations on CLV/ROI bars, live “locking” or “settling” indicators, confetti or subtle success states when a pick grades green.
Visual hierarchy & color system: stronger use of green (positive CLV/yield), red (drawdowns/losses), accent for “verified” badges and rising indicators. Card-based layouts with soft shadows, better typography scale, and breathing room.
Data visualization everywhere: equity curves, rolling ROI, CLV scatter or distribution charts, sport/market breakdown pie or bars, form guide (W/L streak). These turn dry numbers into something engaging.
Micro-interactions: follow button animation, subscribe modal with clear pricing tiers and what you get, toast notifications for new locked picks from followed tipsters.
Dark/light mode toggle (betting sites often lean dark; give choice).
Trust signals: prominent “How verification works” explainer (you already have good copy—surface it better with icons/steps), badges for “Verified”, “Rising”, “Proven sample size”, “Positive CLV streak”.
Loading states and skeletons instead of plain “Loading verified tipsters…”.
Consistent iconography and sport logos (NBA, soccer leagues, etc.).

3. Bettor (consumer) experience improvements

Personal feed of locked picks from followed/subscribed tipsters, with filters (sport, time until kickoff, market).
One-click or clear path to see closing odds vs tipster odds and actual CLV after settlement.
Notifications (email/push/WhatsApp you already link) for new locked picks, settlements, and performance milestones.
Portfolio view: track your own hypothetical or actual bankroll based on followed tips (optional stake input).
Comparison tool: side-by-side tipsters.
Transparency: show exact lock time, hash reference (or truncated), and settlement source.
Onboarding flow for new bettors explaining CLV, why positive CLV > high win rate, and how the hashing works.

4. Tipster experience & incentives

Better tipster onboarding / verification flow (current signup is minimal). Guide them through first picks, profile setup, pricing, and how payouts work.
Tipster dashboard: submit picks easily (with market/odds/stake), see pending/locked/settled, analytics (their own CLV, ROI by market), subscriber management, earnings.
Monetization clarity: transparent revenue share, payout schedule, tools to set weekly/monthly pricing or tiers.
Discovery boosts for new tipsters (featured “Rising”, challenges, or free-trial periods for subscribers).
Community features: comments on settled picks (moderated), tipster Q&A, or private notes for subscribers.
Tools to help tipsters improve (e.g., historical closing line data access, market suggestions).

5. Sports coverage expansion

Currently almost exclusively basketball (NBA-style) and soccer. Expand aggressively to:
Football/soccer (major leagues + cups)
NBA / college basketball / Euroleague
NFL / college football
Tennis (ATP/WTA)
Baseball (MLB)
Hockey (NHL)
Esports (CS, LoL, etc. if you want volume)
Horse racing / other high-interest markets if data allows

Per-sport leaderboards and filters.
Market coverage beyond basic 1X2 / totals: spreads, props, player props, live/in-play if feasible later.
Data partnerships or APIs for reliable results, odds, and closing lines across more sports.
Sport-specific landing pages or sections with relevant tipsters and recent action.
Seasonal emphasis (e.g., highlight NFL during season, March Madness, etc.).

6. Trust, retention, growth & polish

Social proof & activity: “X picks locked in the last 24h”, live activity feed, testimonials once you have them.
Pricing page and clear value props for subscriptions.
Content layer: short explainers, blog posts on “What is CLV?”, case studies of tipsters, responsible gambling messaging.
Performance & SEO: fast loads, proper meta, structured data for tipsters/picks, shareable tipster cards.
Accessibility: good contrast, keyboard nav, screen-reader friendly stats.
Analytics & iteration: track where users drop off (signup, subscribe, follow).
Legal/trust: clear terms, responsible gambling links, verification methodology page.
WhatsApp chat is good—consider in-app support or chatbot later.

The biggest gaps right now are density of content/activity, visual liveliness, discovery tools, and breadth of sports. Fixing the empty/sparse feel and giving both tipsters and bettors clear, delightful daily reasons to return will move the needle most.Prioritize whatever gets real locked picks and engaged users in front of people fastest, then layer the polish and expansion.
